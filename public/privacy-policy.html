1. Introduction

Welcome to delulu ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using delulu, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

Category Data Types Purpose
Account Information Email address, display name, age, password To create and manage your account
Authentication Data Firebase User ID, Google Sign-In credentials (if used) To securely authenticate your identity
Profile Data Profile photo/avatar, dream descriptions To personalize your experience
Progress Data Goals, quests, side quests, tasks, achievements, streaks To track your personal development journey

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

Category Data Types Purpose
Usage Statistics Login history, quests completed, tasks completed, side quests created To display progress and unlock achievements
Device Information Device timezone, notification preferences To schedule local notifications correctly
Purchase Info Subscription status, transaction identifiers To manage premium features via RevenueCat

2.3 User-Generated Content

  • Vision board images (selected from your device gallery)
  • Daily motivation entries & Little wins logs
  • Custom side quests, goals, and dreams

3. How We Use Your Information

  1. Account Management: To create, maintain, and authenticate your user account
  2. Core Functionality: To track your goals, quests, and development progress
  3. Personalization: To customize your experience including themes and content
  4. Premium Services: To process subscriptions and manage premium access
  5. AI-Powered Features: To generate personalized blueprints and motivation content

4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We share data with the following providers who help us operate our App:

4.1 Infrastructure & Auth

Google Firebase: Used for authentication and account management. (Privacy Policy)

MongoDB Atlas: Secure cloud database storage for your goals and progress. (Privacy Policy)

4.2 Payments & Subscriptions

RevenueCat: Manages your subscription status and purchase history. (Privacy Policy)

4.3 AI & Avatars

Google Gemini / Cerebras AI: Used to generate blueprints and motivation. Personal data is processed for context but not stored. (Google | Cerebras)

DiceBear API: Used for generating profile avatars. (Privacy Policy)
