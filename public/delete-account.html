What data will be deleted?

Upon processing your request, we will permanently remove the following information from our servers:

  • Account Profile (Email, Name)
  • Authentication Records
  • Dreams & Vision Board Links
  • Daily Quests & Task History
  • Achievements & Streaks
  • Custom App Settings
  • "Little Wins" Logs
  • AI Interaction History

Data Retention Policy

Once your request is received, your account and data will be deleted within 30 days. If you have an active subscription, we recommend canceling it through the Google Play Store (Settings > Subscriptions) before requesting deletion to avoid further charges.

Note on Purchase History: Transaction identifiers and payment logs related to premium subscriptions are retained for the legally required period (typically 7 years) to comply with tax and audit regulations, though they are decoupled from your personal profile where possible.

Warning: This action is permanent. Once your account and data are deleted, they cannot be recovered, and your progress, achievements, and premium access will be lost.
