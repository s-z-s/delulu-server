# PROJECT CONTEXT: DELULU (THE BLUEPRINT)

## 1. PROJECT META
* **App Name:** Delulu
* **Subtitle:** The Blueprint
* **Tagline:** "The room is empty. See for yourself."
* **Target Audience:** Ambitious women, "Gabby Beckford" fans, aspiring travelers/entrepreneurs.
* **Core Philosophy:** "Delusional Confidence." Life is a Side Quest. Requirements are suggestions. Volume is the strategy (apply to 200 things).
* **Judge/Persona:** Gabby Beckford (PacksLight). The app must embody her specific energy: High-energy, "Six-Figure Solopreneur," "Main Character Energy."

## 2. VOCABULARY & TONE (STRICT RULES)
* **NEVER use:** "Task", "To-Do", "Goal", "Premium", "User".
* **ALWAYS use:**
    * "Side Quest" (instead of Task)
    * "The Blueprint" (instead of Plan)
    * "Blitz" (instead of Start Timer)
    * "Receipts" (instead of History/Log)
    * "The Solulu" (instead of Premium/Subscription)
    * "Main Character" (instead of User)
* **Tone:** Sassy, Encouraging, Hype, Expensive.
    * *Bad:* "Good job completing the task."
    * *Good:* "That's a six-figure move. The room was empty anyway."

## 3. TECH STACK & PACKAGES
* **Framework:** Flutter (Dart) & MERN
* **State Management:** Riverpod
* **Navigation:** GoRouter
* **AI Engine:** `google_generative_ai` (Gemini 2.5 Flash)
* **Monetization:** `purchases_flutter` (RevenueCat)
* **Key UI Packages:**
    * `flutter_card_swiper` (Dashboard Deck)
    * `confetti` (Win Celebration)
    * `screenshot` (Permission Slip Generation)
    * `share_plus` (Sharing Permission Slip)
    * `google_fonts` (Typography)

## 4. DESIGN SYSTEM
* **Primary Color (Delulu Teal):** `#008080` (Buttons, Headers, Brand)
* **Secondary Color (Solulu Coral):** `#FF7F50` (Accents, Blitz Button)
* **Accent Color (Gold):** `#FFD700` (Confetti, Paywall, Stars)
* **Background:** Off-White / Paper texture for cards.
* **Typography:**
    * **Display/Headings:** `League Spartan` (Bold/ExtraBold).
    * **Body:** `Poppins` or `Lato`.
* **Animation Style:** "Juicy." Heavy impact haptics. Thuds, slams, and explosions. No subtle fades.

## 5. FEATURE SPECIFICATIONS

### A. ONBOARDING: "The Transfer"
* **Logic:** A fake loading screen.
* **Visual:** Black background, Neon Teal progress bar.
* **Text Cycle:** "Initializing Audacity..." -> "Deleting Imposter Syndrome..." -> "Transferring Delusion..." -> "100% Loaded."
* **Action:** Transition to `DreamInputScreen`.

### B. DASHBOARD: "The Side Quest Deck"
* **Logic:** Tinder-style card swiper. User only sees ONE micro-action at a time.
* **Card Content:** Generated by Gemini based on user's dream.
* **Swipe Right:** Starts "Blitz Mode" (15-minute timer).
* **Swipe Left:** Skips card.

### C. THE WIN JAR: "Receipts"
* **Trigger:** Floating Action Button (Ticket Icon).
* **Logic:** User manually logs a win (e.g., "Renewed Passport").
* **Reward:**
    * Full-screen Confetti explosion.
    * Sound Effect: Cash Register "Cha-Ching".
    * AI Hype Message (e.g., "Airport lounge access unlocked").

### D. PERMISSION SLIP GENERATOR: "See For Myself"
* **Visual:** Looks like a government certificate or boarding pass.
* **Text:** "OFFICIAL PERMISSION GRANTED TO [Name] TO [Fear]. Signed, The Universe."
* **Animation:** A "YES" stamp scales down rapidly (2.0x to 1.0x) with `HapticFeedback.heavyImpact`.
* **Action:** Save to Gallery / Share to Instagram.

### E. MONETIZATION: "The Solulu Pass"
* **RevenueCat Entitlement ID:** `pro_access`
* **Triggers (When to show Paywall):**
    1.  User tries to "Blitz" a 2nd time in one day (Limit: 1 Blitz/day for free).
    2.  User tries to generate a custom Permission Slip.
* **Copy:** "Gabby applied to 200 scholarships. Real delusion requires VOLUME. Unlock Unlimited Blitz Mode."

## 6. DATA STRUCTURE (FIRESTORE)
* `users/{uid}`
    * `dream`: string
    * `is_pro`: boolean
    * `daily_blitz_count`: int (resets at midnight)
* `users/{uid}/wins`
    * `title`: string
    * `timestamp`: timestamp
    * `ai_hype_comment`: string
* `users/{uid}/quests` (Cache for AI generated quests)
    * `title`: string
    * `duration_minutes`: int (default 15)
    * `status`: 'pending' | 'completed'
