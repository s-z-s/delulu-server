Welcome to Delulu ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using Delulu, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our App.

3. How We Use Your Information

  1. Account Management: To create, maintain, and authenticate your user account
  2. Core App Functionality: To track your goals, quests, achievements, and personal development progress
  3. Personalization: To customize your experience including themes, avatars, and content
  4. Premium Services: To process subscriptions and manage premium feature access
  5. Notifications: To send local reminders about your tasks and goals (only if you grant permission)
  6. AI-Powered Features: To generate personalized blueprints, advice, and motivation content using AI services
  7. Analytics: To understand app usage patterns and improve our services
  8. Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries and provide assistance

6. Data Storage and Security

6.1 Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Cloud Storage: Your data is stored securely on MongoDB Atlas cloud servers and Firebase services
  • Local Storage: Some preferences and cached data are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences
  • Geographic Location: Our backend servers are hosted in secure data centers; specific regions may include the United States and European Union

6.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure token-based authentication
  • Firebase security rules for data access control
  • Regular security audits
